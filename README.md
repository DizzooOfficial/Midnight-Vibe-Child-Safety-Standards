# Child Safety Standards – Midnight Vibe: Stranger Talk

**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026

**App:** Midnight Vibe: Stranger Talk  
**Developer:** Dizzoo Studios  
**Company:** SNGOYAL CONSULTANTS PRIVATE LIMITED  
**Child Safety Contact:** support@dizzoo.com

## 1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Midnight Vibe: Stranger Talk is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse, exploitation, and endangerment.

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Midnight Vibe is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 years and older.

## 2. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

- Child sexual abuse or exploitation in any form.
- Grooming or attempting to exploit minors.
- Sharing, requesting, distributing, or promoting CSAM.
- Sexual harassment, coercion, or exploitation involving minors.
- Sextortion, threats, or blackmail involving children.
- Trafficking or facilitating sexual exploitation of minors.
- Any activity that endangers the safety or well-being of children.

These prohibitions apply to all user profiles, communications, and activities within Midnight Vibe.

## 3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report suspected child exploitation, inappropriate behavior, or child safety concerns through the in-app reporting feature.

Reports may also be submitted by email:

**Email:** support@dizzoo.com  
**Subject:** Child Safety Report – Midnight Vibe

Please provide relevant details to help identify the reported account or incident. Do not send or redistribute illegal child sexual abuse material.

## 4. Review and Enforcement

Reports involving suspected CSAE or CSAM are treated as serious safety concerns.

When violations are identified, appropriate measures may include:

- Restricting or suspending accounts.
- Permanently banning accounts involved in child exploitation.
- Removing prohibited content or associated information where applicable.
- Preserving relevant information where legally required.
- Cooperating with lawful investigations.

We do not tolerate the use of Midnight Vibe for activities that exploit or endanger children.

## 5. Compliance With Child Safety Laws

Midnight Vibe is committed to complying with applicable child protection laws and regulations.

Confirmed incidents involving CSAM or child sexual exploitation will be reported to relevant regional or national authorities where required by applicable law.

We cooperate with valid legal requests and appropriate child protection investigations.

## 6. Age Restrictions

Midnight Vibe is intended only for users aged 18 or older.

Users under 18 are not permitted to create or maintain accounts.

Accounts identified as belonging to minors may be restricted or removed in accordance with our policies.
